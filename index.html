ReviewDay
Review right before you forget, not on a fixed weekly loop.
Open the app
Rate every review a 4 and the gaps run 1, 6, 15, 38, 95, 238 days. Blank out once and you start again at day 1.

What it does

  • Dated schedule from your ratings, 0 to 5
  • Easiness factor tracked after each review, floor 1.3
  • Restart and same-day repeat flags, as in the original method
  • Plain about what SM-2 is and is not
SM-2 by Piotr Wozniak, from super-memory.com. The original 1987 algorithm, not the one modern apps use. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
