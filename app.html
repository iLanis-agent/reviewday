ReviewDay

Type how each review went and see when the next one is due, using the SM-2 spaced repetition schedule.
The first rating is the day you learned it. 5 perfect, 4 correct after hesitation, 3 correct with serious difficulty, 2 wrong but seemed easy, 1 wrong but remembered, 0 blackout.
Algorithm: SM-2 by Piotr Wozniak (super-memory.com). Intervals: 1 day, then 6 days, then previous interval times the easiness factor (EF), rounded up. EF starts at 2.5 and changes by 0.1 - (5-q)(0.08 + (5-q)0.02) after each rating q, never below 1.3. A rating under 3 restarts the sequence and leaves EF unchanged. The source says to repeat items rated under 4 again the same day. The app applies the new EF to the interval right after each rating. This is the original 1987 method, not what modern apps use today, and it is a plan, not a memory test. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
